## Arwen rules the upstream contradicts

1. **The third mono/utility face** (`build.md:51-53`, SKILL.md:86). Upstream removed the "utility face for captions or data" role that its `423563` revision had, and now lists small mono data labels as template chrome (fd:43). **Upstream is right** for brand work: mono labels are one of the visible marks of the editorial-typographic lane that arwen already rejects (SKILL.md:216). Arwen is right only for literal code, IDs and numeric columns in the product register, and `tabular-nums` handles numeric columns without a mono face. Keep mono as a Deliberate exception, not a plan slot.
2. **"Never a page with zero motion"** (craft.md:74-75), and the MOTION 8–10 "ambient" band (SKILL.md:121). Upstream moved in the opposite direction: `423563` said "Leverage motion… ambient atmosphere", and `dbdd79c` says non-user-triggered motion should be used sparingly. **Upstream is right on the default.** Zero ambient motion is a legitimate result, and motion that answers user input is the part worth keeping. Arwen's dial can keep 8–10 for campaign briefs that ask for it, but the "never zero" sentence should go.
3. **Gradient mesh / grain as "depth over flat fills"** (craft.md:93-94) versus "gradient washes as decoration" in the SaaS kit (fd:42). **Upstream is right.** Arwen's caveat says not to use them by reflex, but the sentence still lists them as the recommended answer.
4. **Brief-less subject: decide or confirm.** Upstream says to propose the subject and "confirm with the client" (fd:13). Arwen asks one question only when the ambiguity changes the whole design, and otherwise decides and declares (SKILL.md:139-150). **Both are right.** A brief with no subject *is* whole-design ambiguity, so arwen also asks. Arwen additionally covers the subagent case, which upstream does not address.
5. **Arwen inherited text that upstream has since deleted.** `build.md:42-43` ("only show the user something you're confident about") and `build.md:82-83` ("follow it exactly, deriving every color and type value") are `423563` sentences that `dbdd79c` removed (inferred from matching text against both revisions). They do not conflict with the new upstream in substance, but they show arwen was absorbed from the stale local install.
